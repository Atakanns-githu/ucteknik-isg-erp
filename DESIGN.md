# Design System: Üçteknik İSG-ERP

## Overview
- **Creative North Star:** "Üçteknik çalışma alanı" — white structural navigation, pale neutral canvas (`#f3f5f5`), navy actions (`#12364b` / `#0A2540`) and restrained operational teal selection (`#087f72`).
- **Typography:** Locally hosted Noto Sans (Regular 400 & Bold 700) with Turkish glyph fidelity and tabular numerals in tables.
- **Layout:** Office shell has fixed left rail (`264px`), collapsible rail (`80px`), sticky toolbar (`>=72px`), centered container (`1560px`). Mobile (`<=767px`) switches to card views (`md:hidden`) and fixed bottom task tabs (`>=68px`) with zero horizontal overflow (`390×844`).
- **Accessibility:** WCAG 2.1 AA contrast, native `<dialog>` modals with focus containment and `Escape` dismissal, global `2px` teal keyboard focus rings, and explicit text/semantic connectivity indicators.
